## Installation Notes

1. Disconnect the printer/device from AC power before installation.
2. Mount the unit securely with adequate clearance for ventilation.
3. Connect AC input and DC output terminals per polarity markings.
4. Verify voltage/current settings before powering on.
5. Ground the unit properly; avoid humid or wet environments.
6. Periodically check and tighten terminal screws.
7. Do not exceed rated power/voltage limits.
