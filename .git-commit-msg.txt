Senior-engineer validation pass on chip-database: 6 corrections found and fixed

Re-read every one of the 32 chip-database dossiers with technical audit
scrutiny: checked every formula dimensionally, cross-checked part-number
suffixes against claimed speed grades, checked cross-file consistency,
and flagged any number not backed by a directly-quoted source.

Corrections made:
- Lattice LFXP3C: removed an incorrect "Master SPI" configuration mode
  claim. LatticeXP self-configures from internal flash; Master SPI
  (reading config from an external flash at boot) contradicts that
  architecture and looks like conflation with a different Lattice
  family from one of the cross-referenced AI reports.
- TI DRV8818: the claimed "upgrade path from DRV8811 (lower RDS-on)"
  had an unconfirmed and likely backward direction. Reworded to state
  only the confirmed fact (pin-compatible siblings) and flag the
  improvement direction as unverified.
- Sipex SP29302: the most substantive finding. The original 26W vs 62W
  worked example implied 26W was a safe dissipation level. Running the
  package's own thermal resistance (31.2 C/W) against 26W gives a
  physically impossible temperature rise - the real free-air-only safe
  ceiling is roughly 4W. Added the corrected thermal analysis showing
  this regulator needs real heatsinking at any input voltage, not only
  in the wrong-voltage failure case, and that load current matters as
  much as input voltage.
- Cosmo 357NT: flagged an operating temperature range that closely
  matched a different, unrelated part (AO4606) elsewhere in this
  database - likely cross-contamination during the original synthesis.
- TI TLC5620: flagged the max clock rate figure as not independently
  re-verified this session, unlike every other row in that table.
- TI AM26LV32: flagged a specific dynamic power dissipation figure as
  indicative-only, not independently re-derived.

Also added an explicit confidence footnote to the AM26LV31 pinout table
distinguishing the 14 directly-sourced pins from the 2 inferred-by-
elimination power pins.

Full audit trail, methodology, and a table of spot-checks that passed
(part-number-to-speed-grade consistency, formula arithmetic, etc.) is in
the new chip-database/VALIDATION-REPORT.md. Overall verdict: the
knowledge base is fundamentally sound - 6 of 32 dossiers needed a fix,
none invalidated a chip's core identification or board role.
