Correct machine identification and add manufacturer-confirmed spec sheet

The machine was misidentified throughout this project as "Julong GDH122SA".
Corrected per the owner to: Computerized Flat Knitting Machine, model
GD-H122S, manufactured by Shaoxing Jinhao Machinery Co., Ltd. (Shaoxing,
Zhejiang, China). Replaced in all 8 affected files (README.md and six
docs/ files), with the historical misattribution noted explicitly rather
than silently erased.

This unlocked real manufacturer research this session - three independent
listings (manufacturer showroom on made-in-china.com, a reseller spec
page, and company directory listings) cross-confirm published
specifications for this exact model, none of which could be derived from
the boards alone:

- Needle selection: "full-jacquard solenoid selection" - the first
  direct confirmation of the missing selector board's actuator type
  (solenoid, not piezo or another mechanism), narrowing what any future
  re-engineering effort needs to characterise
- USB data input - independently corroborates the USB host port already
  found on the HMI board
- Take-down: 31 automatically adjustable levels - a concrete candidate
  for what the Main Board's TLC5620 DAC may be setting
- 16 yarn carriers, belt-drive AC servo motor, 100-level electronic
  stitch density, motor-driven racking, stop-motion detection for yarn
  breaks/knots/shocks/program errors
- Power: majority of sources say 3-phase AC 220V; one reseller listing
  says single-phase - recorded as an unresolved discrepancy rather than
  picked one way
- Manufacturer contact found: Keyan Industrial Area, Shaoxing, phone
  0575-84291942, official site jindajx.com (blocked automated fetch,
  worth a direct visit) - a real, findable lead for spares and manuals,
  not just a scrapped-machine search
